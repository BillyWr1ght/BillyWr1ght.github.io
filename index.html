Project Overview

This project is a multi-joint robotic arm built around 3D-printed structural parts and servo actuation. The goal is to create a compact arm that can move reliably while keeping the structure lightweight, printable, and easy to revise.

The most important part of the project is not just making the arm move. It is designing each joint so the CAD geometry, printed parts, servo torque, and assembly tolerances work together as one system.

Model joints and linkages as a complete moving assembly in CAD.
Design printable servo mounts with practical clearances and fastener access.
Reduce flex and unnecessary mass where torque demands are highest.
Test physical motion, identify weak points, and revise the design.
Current Design Focus
Shoulder torqueThe largest load occurs when the arm is extended.
Joint stiffnessPrinted links must resist flex without adding excessive weight.
Servo integrationMounts, clearances, fasteners, and range of motion must work together.
